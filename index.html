Prototype writeup

What have we done so far?

So far, we have collected and cleaned the national FRED data and ACS data, which covered 414 metropolitan areas. Additionally, we have built a Python script to standardize the metro names and prepare the datasets for the website. We have also begun creating our responsive and interactive D3.js webpage. So far, we have added the following features: a year slider for rebasing national affordability trends, a city dropdown for comparing rent burdens, and a scatterplot comparing rent and income growth across the metros. We also designed the site’s “and-but-therefore” aspect.

What will be the most challenging of your project to design and why?

The most challenging part we anticipate designing will be making historical comparisons geographically and economically fair. Additionally, we find that handling changing census geography may be a challenge, as metropolitan boundaries differ over time, making previous data less reliable. We also anticipate that choosing the right affordability measure will be difficult, as the median household rent burden does not accurately represent a 22-year-old's circumstances. Finally, we anticipate a challenge when designing a down payment calculator. The calculator will require savings rates, inflation, assumptions, and potentially changing housing costs. The challenge will arise when ensuring these assumptions remain visible and adjustable rather than presented as one fixed answer.
